# Assembly photography

Two original fictional venue images were generated with the built-in image
generation tool for this template on 26 October 2026. They depict no real named
venue or speakers. Replace them with verified event photography for a real event.

The full resolution originals are retained in the local workspace at
`docs/backgrounds/assembly-hall.png` and `docs/backgrounds/assembly-venue.png`.
Only compressed JPEG copies ship with the template:

| Asset | Use | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hall.jpg` | Wide hero photograph | Sunlit converted brick waterfront conference hall, tall black steel windows, industrial roof trusses, long communal tables, orange chairs and candid adult attendees. Quiet documentary photography, warm natural light, no signs, logos or baked-in typography. |
| `venue.jpg` | Venue guide | Matching fictional converted brick waterfront venue exterior in Rotterdam, tall black steel windows, large open orange entrance, bicycles and two people. Architectural documentary photography, natural light, no signs, logos or baked-in typography. |

Both were made using built-in generation mode rather than an external image API.
The warm brick, orange seating and orange entrance intentionally connect the two
photographs to Assembly's vermilion palette. Source images are not reused by any
other Senn template.
